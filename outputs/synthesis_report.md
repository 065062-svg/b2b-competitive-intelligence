### Competitive Intelligence Synthesis Report

#### 1. Executive Summary
This report consolidates the competitive intelligence gathered from the Scout Research Report for Zoho CRM, Salesforce, HubSpot, and Freshworks. It provides a structured comparison of their products, features, pricing, and market activities, supported by documented evidence. The report highlights key similarities and differences, identifies market patterns, and points out information gaps. The findings are based solely on the information provided in the Scout Report.

#### 2. Research Scope and Evidence Limitations
The research scope includes product updates, feature enhancements, pricing changes, and market expansion activities for Zoho CRM, Salesforce, HubSpot, and Freshworks. The report does not introduce additional competitors. The evidence limitations are noted for competitors with incomplete or unclear information, such as specific pricing details and market expansion activities.

More details can be found [here](https://www.zoho.com/blog/crm/q1-2026-update.html) and [here](https://www.zoho.com/en-us/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html).

Pricing details can be found [here](https://www.zoho.com/en-us/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html) and [here](https://www.zoho.com/crm/whats-new/release-notes.html).

##### Salesforce
- **Product Updates and Enhancements:** Salesforce has released updates in the Spring and Summer 2026 releases. The latest updates can be found [here](https://www.salesforce.com/products/innovation/spring-2026-release/) and [here](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/summer-2026-product-release-announcement/).
- **Pricing Changes:** Salesforce has increased CRM pricing in 2026. The pricing plans are now $180/user/month for Essentials, $200/user/month for Professional, and $40/user/month for Enterprise. More details can be found [here](https://www.getpricepulse.com/companies/salesforce-pricing.html) and [here](https://costbench.com/software/crm/salesforce/).

##### HubSpot
- **Product Updates and Enhancements:** HubSpot has announced product updates for July 2026, including new features for Customer Agent coaching tools and expanded HubSpot connectors. More details can be found [here](https://community.hubspot.com/t/july-2026-product-updates/155091) and [here](https://www.hubspot.com/new).
- **Pricing Changes:** HubSpot has not provided specific pricing changes for 2026. However, the company has released a guide on how much a Zoho consultant costs in 2026, covering engagement economics alongside licence costs. More details can be found [here](https://lets-viz.com/blogs/zoho-crm-pricing-plans-compared-standard-to-ultimate-2026).

##### Freshworks
- **Product Updates and Enhancements:** Freshworks has announced product updates for September 2026, including the release of AI Agent Studio in Freshservice, which aims to unlock service transformation and business growth. More details can be found [here](https://www.freshworks.com/theworks/company-news/september-2026-freshworks-innovation-update/) and [here](https://www.freshworks.com/freshservice/product-updates/).
- **Pricing Changes:** Freshworks has not provided specific pricing changes for 2026. However, Freshdesk and Freshsales pricing plans have been updated. Freshdesk pricing starts at $15 and Freshsales pricing starts from $9 USD. More details can be found [here](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/) and [here](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/pricing/).

#### 5. Key Similarities and Differences
- **Similarities:** All four CRM competitors have focused on improving compliance, field productivity, service efficiency, and industry-specific workflows. They have also introduced new features and updates to their products.
- **Differences:** Salesforce has increased CRM pricing, while Zoho CRM has not provided specific pricing changes. HubSpot has not provided specific pricing changes, but has released a guide on how much a Zoho consultant costs in 2026. Freshworks has not provided specific pricing changes, but has updated Freshdesk and Freshsales pricing plans.

#### 6. Evidence-Supported Market Patterns
- **Industry Developments:** Salesforce has introduced innovations to improve compliance, field productivity, service efficiency, and industry-specific workflows. This suggests that compliance and industry-specific workflows are important market trends.
- **Emerging Trends:** AI and service transformation are emerging trends in the CRM market, as indicated by Freshworks' AI Agent Studio in Freshservice and HubSpot's new features for Customer Agent coaching tools.
