- **Customer Engagement Focus**: The platform emphasizes personalized customer engagement, which can be a key differentiator in the market.

**Weaknesses:**

- **Limited Market Presence**: Zoho CRM may not have the same level of market presence and brand recognition as Salesforce, which could affect its market share and customer acquisition.

**Opportunities:**

**Threats:**
