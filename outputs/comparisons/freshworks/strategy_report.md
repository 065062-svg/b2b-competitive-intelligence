### Executive Strategic Intelligence Brief for Zoho CRM

#### 1. Executive Summary

This executive strategic intelligence brief synthesizes the findings from the Scout Research Report for Zoho CRM and Freshworks. Both companies have introduced new features and updates, and offer different pricing tiers. The report highlights the similarities and differences in their product offerings, pricing strategies, and market positioning. Zoho CRM should consider leveraging its free tier to attract more SMBs and differentiate itself from Freshworks. Freshworks should continue to focus on its differentiated features and pricing tiers to maintain its market position and customer retention.
