#### 8. Potential Implications for the Focal Company (Analysis)

- **Competitive Landscape:** Zoho CRM and Freshworks are both strong players in the CRM software market, offering similar features and pricing structures. However, Zoho CRM has a free tier for up to 3 users, which could be a competitive advantage in attracting smaller businesses. Freshworks' unified authentication presets and stronger data integrity for contacts and companies are notable features that could differentiate them from Zoho CRM.
- **Market Positioning:** Both companies have maintained their market positions with regular updates and pricing strategies. Zoho CRM's free tier for up to 3 users could position it as a more accessible solution for SMBs, while Freshworks' differentiated features and pricing tiers could position them as a more comprehensive solution for larger enterprises.
- **Customer Acquisition and Retention:** The presence of a free tier for Zoho CRM and the variety of pricing tiers for Freshworks could impact customer acquisition and retention strategies. Zoho CRM's free tier could attract more users, while Freshworks' differentiated features and pricing tiers could help retain existing customers by offering tailored solutions.

#### 9. Conclusion

The competitive landscape for CRM software is characterized by regular updates and pricing strategies from both Zoho CRM and Freshworks. Zoho CRM's free tier for up to 3 users and Freshworks' unified authentication presets and stronger data integrity for contacts and companies are notable features that could differentiate them in the market. The focal company, Zoho CRM, should consider leveraging its free tier to attract more SMBs and differentiate itself from Freshworks. Freshworks should continue to focus on its differentiated features and pricing tiers to maintain its market position and customer retention.
